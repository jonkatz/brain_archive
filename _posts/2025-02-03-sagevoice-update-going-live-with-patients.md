## Goals for February

My number one goal is and has been to find an easily identifiable customer, with a prioritized problem that I am well positioned to solve. I have some leads and my goal in February is to get further with my validation.

I have a couple of use cases I am still feeling out. They’re at different stages.

### Medical facilities (20% of my energy)

**Stage 1: Validated a painful problem**

I have heard this is an expensive problem, but not sure if people will pay for it or that I have the right solution

- It is clear that there is a pain point around medical adherence following patient discharge, particularly by ACOs (who get paid a lump fee for each patient and each re-admittance is money out of their pocket) and Skilled Nursing Facilities (who get reimbursement penalties for readmittance)
- The big risk is that I don’t have a channel for reaching them (founder/market fit) or there isn’t enough system incentive to solve this problem.

**Current activity:** I am trying to find someone who will talk to me from one of these places.

**Goal:** get 3-4 conversations with folks this month.  I have a couple warm intros I am banking on, but will also do some outreach.

### Clinical research (80% of my energy)

**Stage 2: Validated there is a problem, people are willing to pay for it (or so they say with great enthusiasm).**

- I have validated that these are problems that cost a great deal of money, I can find the people who care, and we are testing if my solution can solve them.
- The pain points identified so far are around subject screening, survey delivery and interviews.
- The big risk is that subjects wont talk to the AI or the IRB (ethics committee for research) creates a lot of friction.

**Current activity:** getting pilots off the ground to validate that it works

**Goal:** Get feedback from 4 different (interested) research groups. Have live subjects using this by the end of the month, have HIPAA compliance by the end of the month, which is a big barrier to scale.

### Hipaa compliance

- My number two goal is around getting either HIPAA Compliant or convincingly close enough to make progress.  Nobody can run any sizeable pilots with me until we have that.
- The bulk of this is moving all of my stack to Amazon, which has HIPAA compliance more or less baked into it.
- The part I was most worried about was the AI itself. On Friday, however, the foundational AI I use signed a BAA for me. A BAA basically means that my usage of them is now HIPAA compliant. 😅
- There aren’t any real barriers now except time.
