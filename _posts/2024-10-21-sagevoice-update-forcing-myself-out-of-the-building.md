**This Week: Outreach**

As an early-stage founder, talking to users is the thing I am most likely to under-invest on. So without application deadlines, or significant software issues to fix, I’m going to try and focus on outreach to set up conversations.

* I know the current framing is an obstacle for caregivers concerned about their parents. I want to get feedback from potential users on some potential solutions. It’s time to get further outside my comfort zone to recruit people. I’d like to set up 10 interviews for the next 2 weeks.
* I still have only talked to a few people at senior living facilities. They’re hard to reach. I had a goal of talking to 3 this month, and I’ve talked to 2.  I’m increasing the goal up to 4.
* I started some co-founder outreach last week. My goal is to reach out to 5 people each week for a while and see what that leads to.
